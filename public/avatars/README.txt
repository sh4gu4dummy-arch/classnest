ClassNest Avatar Packs
======================
These images ship with ClassNest and work fully offline once the app is published.

kids/   — 40 kid-friendly mascots (01.jpg … 40.jpg)
teens/  — 40 cooler power characters: fighters, wild animals, sci-fi, human-like, mythic

Each pack has its own manifest.json (id, name, path).

Avatar pack is chosen per class (Kids or Teens) when creating a class or in Settings.

Evolution: every 10 points a student unlocks a stronger power tier + celebration animation.

Use "Download portable kit" in the app to get both packs plus your classroom backup.
