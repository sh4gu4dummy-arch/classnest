# Ultra lore bible (audit)

**Purpose:** Every Ultra has a real story so Home / intro / adventure films mean something — not random motion.  
**Do not shoot films from this file until the teacher says `go`.**  
**Do not delete current mp4s without a yes.**

Short lines still in the app: `src/lib/ultra-lore.ts` (home, greeting) and `src/lib/ultra-adventures.ts` (5-beat adventure). This bible is the **longer origin + Home-film seed**. After audit, we can lift paragraphs into the catalog.

**Video rules (all future Home films):** 15s, sound, one hero, 3 beats, one **object** prop, mid-shot, face readable, no humans, no extra copies. Recipe: [ultra-home-films.md](ultra-home-films.md). QA stills: [home-film-qa/NOTES.md](home-film-qa/NOTES.md).

Each entry:

- **Virtue** — what the class is practicing when this legend is on the board  
- **Story** — origin (classroom-safe)  
- **Home film** — 3 beats + prop for a future 15s clip in that home  

---

## 01 Aetherflame — Crystal lightning wolf
**Home:** The Glass Spire  
**Virtue:** Tell the truth when it is hard  
**Story:** Born the night two storms shook hands on a glass mountain. The pack says his mane only lights if someone speaks true. Liars see only weather. He is proud, not cruel — the mountain answers honesty with a path of frozen lightning.  
**Home film:** Walks the ridge in a spark-blizzard → catches **one bolt** that becomes a lantern → sets it on a cairn.  
**Prop:** lantern-bolt. **Don't:** extra wolves, extra legs.
